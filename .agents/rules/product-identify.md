HueViVu is NOT:
- a booking platform
- a tourism listing app
- an admin dashboard
- a generic travel template

HueViVu IS:
“An AI-powered cultural travel companion for exploring Hue emotionally and intelligently.”

The product must always feel:
- cinematic
- emotionally warm
- calm
- premium
- immersive
- intelligent
- adaptive

The user should feel:
“An AI is personally guiding my journey through Hue.”

NOT:
“I am browsing tourism data.”

--------------------------------------------------

CORE EXPERIENCE PHILOSOPHY

Every screen must:
- reduce stress
- encourage curiosity
- feel breathable
- feel emotionally guided
- feel atmospheric

Avoid:
- dashboard structures
- enterprise UI feeling
- rigid layouts
- generic travel app patterns
- excessive complexity

--------------------------------------------------

AI COMPANION PHILOSOPHY

The AI is:
- warm
- observant
- intelligent
- adaptive
- emotionally aware

The AI is NOT:
- a chatbot
- a support center
- robotic automation

GOOD:
“I planned your morning around cooler weather and quieter streets ✨”

BAD:
“Route optimized.”

--------------------------------------------------

CULTURAL ATMOSPHERE

Hue influence must be:
- subtle
- elegant
- atmospheric

Express Hue through:
- lighting
- spacing
- calmness
- imperial elegance
- riverside atmosphere
- warm sunset tones

Avoid:
- cliché tourism graphics
- overdecorated Vietnamese elements
- cartoon local motifs

Hue should be FELT emotionally,
not explicitly pasted everywhere.

--------------------------------------------------

FINAL EXPERIENCE TARGET

The final product should feel like:
“Apple Travel meets AI-guided cultural exploration in Hue.”

Before generating ANY screen, ask:
- Does this feel cinematic?
- Does this feel emotionally warm?
- Does this feel like Hue specifically?
- Does the AI feel alive?
- Does this feel like a premium AI travel companion?

If not:
redesign it.
