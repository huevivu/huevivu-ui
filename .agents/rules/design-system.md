UX FLOW PHILOSOPHY

The experience should feel:
- adaptive
- nonlinear
- exploration-based
- intelligent

Avoid:
- rigid next-next-next progression
- forced onboarding
- excessive friction

Users should feel:
“I control the journey.”

--------------------------------------------------

ITINERARY RULES

The itinerary should feel:
“A guided adventure.”

NOT:
“A calendar schedule.”

Each day should include:
- emotional subtitle
- atmosphere banner
- narrative pacing
- contextual AI insights

Example:
“Imperial Heritage & Local Flavors”

Narrative example:
“A calm cultural morning followed by hidden local food experiences.”

--------------------------------------------------

TIMELINE RULES

Timelines should feel:
- alive
- cinematic
- story-driven

Use:
- glowing progress paths
- elegant connectors
- atmospheric spacing
- contextual travel moments

Activities should feel:
“part of a real day.”

--------------------------------------------------

AI INTERACTION RULES

The AI should:
- proactively assist
- adapt contextually
- feel conversational
- feel embedded into the experience

Use:
- inline AI suggestions
- contextual prompts
- adaptive recommendations
- floating AI hints

Avoid:
- chatbot support feeling
- robotic prompts
- system-generated tone

--------------------------------------------------

CUSTOMIZATION RULES

Customization must feel:
“like talking with an intelligent travel companion.”

DO NOT use:
- large forms
- technical filters
- settings-heavy panels

Instead use:
- AI suggestion chips
- conversational interactions
- contextual adjustments

Examples:
- Less walking
- More hidden gems
- More local food
- Relaxed schedule
- Add nightlife

--------------------------------------------------

HOME SCREEN RULES

Home should prioritize:
- emotional continuation
- active journey
- contextual discovery
- intelligent suggestions

Home must feel:
“your journey is waiting for you.”

--------------------------------------------------

EXPLORE SCREEN RULES

Explore should feel:
- visual
- immersive
- map-aware
- discovery-focused

Exploration should feel:
“walking through Hue digitally.”

--------------------------------------------------

MOTION & INTERACTION RULES

Interactions should feel:
- soft
- responsive
- intentional
- emotionally smooth

Use:
- gentle scaling
- soft transitions
- contextual reveals
- cinematic pacing

Avoid:
- abrupt changes
- hyperactive animation
- cluttered interaction flows
